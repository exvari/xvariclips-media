## What this batch taught me

1. **The channel list was the problem, not the week.** The 34 "entertainer" channels I had been searching produce top clips that are overwhelmingly sexual content, because that is what gets clipped on those streams. Searching the **GTA VI category itself** (all channels) and mid-size streamers — ohnePixel, LIRIK, MissMikkaa, joe_bartolozzi, distortion2, avoidingthepuddle, theBurntPeanut, 2xRakai, ramez05 — produced ten real candidates in one pass, after two prior passes produced one.
2. **The old gate was too narrow.** `taste.md` required "a specific quotable line + loud reaction". That rule rejects discoveries (#3, #4), physics and NPC behaviour (#7), animal chaos (#5), and deadpan (#9) — all of which the new brief explicitly wants. Gate is rewritten.
3. **Where the funny lives has moved.** Week one of a new game is about *finding out what the game does*. "Wait, you can do that?" is currently a stronger lane for us than "streamer yells".
4. **Viewer clips cut off the payoff.** #1's best line is 50 seconds *after* the clip ends. Pulling the VOD window is now a default step for any finalist, not a fallback.
